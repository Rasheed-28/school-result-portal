School Academic & Grading Guidelines

Passing Standards:
• Section A: 70% minimum passing mark.
• Section B / Boys / Girls: 40% minimum passing mark.
• Failed Subject Policy: Marks obtained in failed subjects are strictly excluded from the student's Total Marks.
Grading Scale & Teacher Remarks:
• ≤ 100%: Grade A+ | Remarks: Excellent
• ≤ 90%: Grade A | Remarks: Very Good
• ≤ 80%: Grade B | Remarks: Good
• ≤ 70%: Grade C | Remarks: Satisfactory
• ≤ 60%: Grade D | Remarks: Work Hard
• ≤ 50%: Grade E | Remarks: Poor
• < 40%: Grade F | Remarks: Fail
